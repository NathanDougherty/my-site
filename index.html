About Me

Hi, I'm Nathanael Dougherty, a 20-year-old student based in Boston, Massachusetts. I'm studying Computer Science and Finance at Northeastern University, and I expect to graduate in December 2027. I chose this combination because I've always been drawn to two things: building software that works well and understanding how markets move. Over time I realized those interests fit together naturally, since modern finance runs on code, data, and systems that have to be both fast and reliable.

My focus is building fast, reliable systems at the intersection of software and quantitative finance. I enjoy problems where performance really matters, where a well-designed program can process huge amounts of market data, test an idea, or make a decision in a fraction of a second. I'm also interested in the math behind finance, like estimating returns, measuring risk, and understanding how assets move together.

I interned as a software engineer on the trading operations team at Flow Traders, and this spring I'll be joining a bank as a trader. These experiences let me see firsthand how technology and finance come together in a real-world setting. That experience has pushed me to keep growing as both an engineer and a finance student. Outside of work and class, I like learning new tools, working on projects, and exploring how data can tell a clear story. This website is a small look at who I am and what I'm passionate about.
